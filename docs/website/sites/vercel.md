# Vercel (https://vercel.com)

## Hero
- Headline: "Agentic Infrastructure"
- Subhead: none under the headline. Three short lines sit on the right: "For coding agents / To ship apps and agents / Automated by agents" (the DOM expands each one, e.g. "with Vercel's API, CLI, MCP, and Skills").
- Primary CTA: "Deploy now" (black pill, links to /signup). Secondary: "Talk to sales" (white, links to /contact/sales). The nav also offers "Get a Demo", "Log In" and "Sign Up".
- Visual: the black Vercel triangle, centred with a soft glow (2 canvases on the page). The DOM also contains a "Drop to deploy" affordance, so the hero is a drag-and-drop deploy target.

## Structure
1. Hero: headline, two CTAs, triangle, three-line value list.
2. Full-width logo row: Meta, DoorDash, OpenAI, SpaceX, The Weather Company, Polymarket.
3. "Build agents on infrastructure that thinks like them": a Notion AI chat mock, the line "Notion powers millions of agent conversations daily on Vercel", and a Features list (Durable Orchestration, Sandboxed Environments, AI Model Gateway, Fluid Compute).
4. "Ship apps that scale from zero to millions instantly": a Zapier site screenshot, "Zapier serves over 100 million monthly website visits", and a Features list. This appeared in the original capture but not in the slow-scroll recapture, so it probably rotates or is A/B tested.
5. "Recently shipped": a bento of three cards (Backends, Agents with chat bubbles, Sandbox).
6. Closing CTA "Built by you, or your agents" with "Deploy now" and "Onboard your agent" (a copy button to paste a prompt into your coding agent).
7. Mega footer with a live "ALL SYSTEMS NORMAL" status and a theme switch.

## Proof
- Logo row directly under the hero.
- Each feature block is anchored on one customer with one big number ("millions of agent conversations daily", "100 million monthly website visits").
- No testimonials, benchmarks or star counts on the homepage.

## Product shown how
Barely shown. The homepage shows customer products (a Notion AI chat UI, the Zapier website), not the Vercel dashboard, and there's no code. Large areas are scroll-triggered animations that stayed blank in the headless captures.

## Visual style
- Type: Geist Sans at 64px, weight 400, letter-spacing −3.84px (very tight, light weight). Geist Mono for the status line.
- Colour: off-white #FAFAFA background, near-black #171717 buttons and text, one blue accent (#0070F3-ish) on the Backends card. Almost monochrome.
- Light and extremely sparse, with huge white gaps.
- Motion: 2 canvases plus scroll-triggered reveals.
- Signature devices: the glowing logo-as-hero, CTAs styled as pills, customer-metric captions in two-tone grey and black text, and the status badge in the footer.

## Pricing / comparison pages
- No pricing or comparison pages captured. "Pricing" sits in the top nav next to "Enterprise".
- Docs link is only in the footer ("Learn → Docs"), not the top nav.

## What makes it sell
- "Deploy now" as the primary action is a verb-first, instant-gratification CTA, backed by a real drop-to-deploy hero.
- "Onboard your agent / Paste to your agent" treats the visitor's coding agent as a buyer, which is very current.
- Each section pairs one customer with one huge number.
- The Meta/OpenAI/SpaceX logo row gives instant enterprise credibility.

## Weaknesses
- The headline is a category slogan and doesn't say what the product does. It relies on brand recognition that a new project doesn't have.
- No product UI, code or pricing signal on the homepage, and a lot of empty space (blank animated regions in both captures).

## Steal for cetra
- Make the hero CTA a verb that completes in seconds (e.g. a copyable `curl | sh` or `docker run` command), plus a secondary "paste this into your coding agent" button that copies an install prompt.
- Anchor each feature section on one concrete number (spans/s ingested, ms query latency, MB RAM).
- Show a live "status" or version badge in the footer for a heartbeat feel.

## Scores
- Beauty 4/5: elegant Geist minimalism, but too empty.
- Selling 3/5: strong CTA verbs, but the vague slogan needs brand equity.
- Clarity 2/5: "Agentic Infrastructure" alone doesn't explain the product.
- Proof 4/5: top-tier logos and big customer numbers.
- Product-showing 1/5: no dashboard, CLI or code shown.
